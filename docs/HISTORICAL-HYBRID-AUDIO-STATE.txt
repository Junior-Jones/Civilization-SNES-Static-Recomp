Civilization Historical Hybrid Audio State
===========================================

Version 36 Part 2 removed the superseded development transport from the
production CivRecomp machine. These fields documented incremental V06-V19
research, but they were no longer read or updated by the authoritative runtime:

- the handwritten CivSmpState and its ARAM-known mask;
- shadow ARAM, DSP registers, known masks and functional timer state;
- APUIO latch mirrors and read/write counters;
- V11 IPL upload/rendezvous bookkeeping;
- V12 driver-rendezvous bookkeeping;
- V13 selector-1 trace storage;
- V14 command burst digests and counters;
- obsolete V15 and V16 transport counters, except the generated main-thread
  v16_wram_clear_bytes diagnostic still written by compact AOT templates;
- V19 selector-$0C upload/command bookkeeping and NMI wait counters;
- the V19 audio-return gate and V20 comparison-lane counters;
- the unused mirror of the current DSP register file.

The production lane is unchanged: civilization_audio.cpp owns the only Full
Static S-SMP/S-DSP route through sc_static_apu. APUIO reads and writes go
directly to that authority; its exact-PC AOT guard remains fail closed. Current
PCM, DSP/ARAM/port digests, snapshot state and failure diagnostics remain.

The legacy v19_audio_return_observed field had no writer anywhere in production
or generated code but gated first-visible-frame capture. Removing the dead gate
makes the real forced-blank-to-visible transition itself arm presentation.

Historical discovery receipts remain under docs/research and the earlier
Version archives. They are evidence, not executable runtime state.
